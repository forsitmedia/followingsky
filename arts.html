Featured Artist · Contemporary Watercolour

Ivone Machado

Portuguese · Lisbon · b. Luanda 1971

Machado works with pigment, water and paper as a system of thought — slow, demanding and irreversible. Her series The Colour of Wisdom – A Portrait of the Future brings together eleven speculative portraits, each built from a single pigment and projected into 2051. Two solo shows in Lisbon and selected for the BIALE International Art Biennial, 2025.
