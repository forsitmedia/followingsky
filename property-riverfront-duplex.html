Property Description

Discover the charm of this magnificent duplex located on the top floor of a recently renovated 18th-century Pombaline building, located on the prestigious Rua Vítor Cordon, in the heart of Chiado, Lisbon. With a total area of 170.97m², this apartment offers a unique and sophisticated living experience.

The spacious living room offers stunning views over the Tagus River, while the contemporary kitchen is equipped with top-of-the-range appliances, ideal for culinary lovers.

The master suite, with integrated closet, and the two spacious bedrooms guarantee comfort and privacy. The bathrooms are elegantly decorated with premium finishes.

Enjoy the terrace and balcony, perfect for moments of relaxation. In addition, the apartment includes a parking space and is equipped with air conditioning and heating by heat/cool pump, ensuring maximum comfort in all seasons.

This exclusive gated community, with doorman, offers security and tranquility, being close to renowned restaurants, exclusive shops and emblematic cultural sites.
