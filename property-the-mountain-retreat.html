Private Luxury Retreat in Arrábida — Exclusive Estate with Heated Pool and Panoramic Views

Discover a slice of paradise on the slopes of the Arrábida Mountains: a traditional estate reimagined with contemporary comfort and luxury finishes, designed for those who value privacy, nature, and a sophisticated lifestyle.

Price: €3,900,000
Gross area: 406 m² (366 m² usable)
Plot: 52,703 m² — a private domain with landscaped gardens and natural surroundings.

The main house blends rustic soul with modern comfort, featuring original wooden beams, stone walls, and large glazed openings that flood the interiors with natural light and frame breathtaking mountain views. Noble materials—wooden flooring and natural stone in the bathrooms—enhance every space.

On the upper floor, a generous south-facing balcony serves a suite and two bedrooms, opening onto serene panoramas. The home offers a perfect balance between generous social areas and cozy private spaces — ideal for family living as well as entertaining guests.

Outdoor living is a constant invitation to relax and celebrate: a heated swimming pool, a spacious covered social area, and lawns stretching through olive groves and pine woodland. There is an annex with en-suite bedrooms (Canadian-style), staff accommodation, and parking for approximately six vehicles.

The estate is fitted with a comprehensive surveillance system, ensuring total peace of mind in a setting of rare privacy — where modern security meets the tranquility of the countryside.

Set in a privileged position within the Arrábida Natural Park, the property enjoys stunning views over the mountains and is just minutes from charming villages, renowned beaches, and regional vineyards — ideal for those seeking an exclusive retreat without compromising accessibility and amenities.

A versatile property with outstanding potential: a primary luxury residence, an exclusive holiday home, a boutique tourism project, or a high-end residential investment. The current layout readily supports hospitality or short-term rental use with complementary services.
