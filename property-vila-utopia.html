Property Description

A home shaped by
life outdoors.

Greenery, generous glazing and warm natural finishes set the tone for this four-suite villa in Vila Utopia. Cork flooring and timber details soften its contemporary architecture, while the living spaces open towards the garden.

Beyond the house, a saltwater pool, outdoor dining area and equipped kitchen invite an easy rhythm of entertaining. A grill and pizza oven sit alongside fruit trees, aromatic planting and raised vegetable beds.

The interior kitchen pairs Häcker cabinetry and Dekton surfaces with Gaggenau appliances, Liebherr refrigeration and a wine cooler. Four suites, five bathrooms, a garage and a laundry room provide space for both family life and guests.

Air conditioning, heating, water filtration and automatic irrigation add everyday convenience. The 653 m² plot accommodates 363 m² of gross construction area, including 262 m² of private gross area. The home can be offered furnished and equipped, subject to agreement.

With access to the A5 and international schools nearby, Vila Utopia connects garden living with the wider Lisbon area. A considered setting for quiet mornings, shared meals and time spent outside.
