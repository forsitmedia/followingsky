About Sharon

Following Sky Real Estate was founded by Sharon Portal, a visionary leader with over 20 years of experience in the real estate industry.

Beyond her expertise in property, Sharon is recognized for her ability to create connections, build synergies, and expand opportunities across people, brands, and investments.

Having built an international career across Africa, Morocco, India, and Europe, she brings a global perspective and a refined understanding of luxury lifestyle, art, and iconic design.

Through Following Sky, Sharon goes beyond real estate — curating exceptional properties, high-value assets, and unique experiences, while delivering a personalized, transparent, and elevated service at every stage of the journey.
