The Artist

Ivone Machado

Portuguese · b. Luanda, 1971 · Based in Lisbon

Ivone Machado works with pigment, water and paper as a system of thought — slow, demanding and irreversible. Trained in Communication Design at the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Lisbon, with an Erasmus exchange at KASK in Ghent, she moved toward painting as a discipline that resists speed.

"Painting is an act of resistance against acceleration."

— Ivone Machado

Her series The Colour of Wisdom – A Portrait of the Future imagines women in the year 2051 — eleven speculative portraits, each conceived from a single pigment, each face projected forward in time. These are not specific people; they are symbolic figures. Each colour becomes a personality, an inner world, an emotional state. The series asks us to see ageing not as loss, but as transformation, strength, and wisdom. Two solo shows in Lisbon in 2025, and selected for the BIALE International Art Biennial, Estremoz.
