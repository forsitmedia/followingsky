Property Description

Lisbon character,
a private garden.

The character of 1950s architecture finds a contemporary expression in this three-storey Restelo residence. Designer renovation works in 2025 bring a new layer to the home, while handmade three-dimensional tiles give its garden-facing spaces a distinctive texture.

Across 710 m² of building area, generous proportions and natural light create a setting for both everyday living and entertaining. Wide sliding doors connect the living and dining rooms, allowing the spaces to flow together when the occasion calls for it.

Five suites are arranged across the upper and lower levels, complemented by a separate staff en-suite room. The principal suite opens directly onto the garden and private swimming pool, bringing a quiet connection to the outdoors into the daily rhythm of the house.

Around the pool, garden living areas offer room to pause, gather and enjoy time outside. Views over the Tagus add another dimension to this Lisbon setting, pairing the city's architectural character with a sense of openness.

Available to rent at €20,000 per month, The Restelo Residence offers space for a considered way of living. Contact Following Sky to discuss the rental and arrange a private viewing.
